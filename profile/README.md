# 🏛️ Cirious Foundation

Building research, systems engineering, and low-level modular technologies centered around Rust 🦀.

---

## 👑 The Founder and the Vision

The **Cirious Foundation** represents the personal and philosophical vision of **Tulio Souza Santos**, founder of the initiative and lead architect behind the projects associated with the Cirious ecosystem.

More than a traditional organization, the Foundation exists as a hub for research, experimentation, and engineering dedicated to the development of modern, high-efficiency software, with a particular focus on:

* Modular architectures
* Low-level systems
* Engines and infrastructure for game development
* Data-oriented performance
* Runtime safety and predictability

Using Rust as its primary engineering language, the Foundation explores modern approaches to building scalable, reliable, and sustainable software for the long term.

---

## 📐 Philosophy

The vision of the Cirious Foundation is guided by a few simple principles:

### ⚡ Efficiency Before Complexity

Designing systems that are clear, predictable, and efficient while avoiding unnecessary abstractions.

### 🧩 Modularity as a Foundation

Building reusable and decoupled components instead of monolithic structures.

### 🛡️ Safety and Reliability

Prioritizing robust and secure architectures designed for modern multi-core environments.

### 🚀 Performance-Oriented Engineering

Exploring low-level techniques, memory organization, and efficient execution models.

---

*Minimalist by design. Consistent in execution.*

*We do not seek merely to write software — we seek to understand and build better systems.*
